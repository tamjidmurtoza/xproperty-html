Living on the Upper West Side

Wedged between Central Park and the Hudson River, the Upper West Side is one of Manhattan's most settled neighborhoods. Pre-war co-ops and brownstones line the side streets, while Broadway, Amsterdam and Columbus Avenues keep groceries, cafes and theaters within a few blocks of every front door.

Families come for the parks and highly rated public schools, and stay for the slower pace. Buyers in 2026 are paying a little more than last year, but well-priced homes still sell in about a month.

Living Here

  • Schools

    Several top-rated public elementary schools, plus well-known private and Montessori options.

  • Transport

    The 1, 2, 3, B and C trains reach Midtown in about 15 minutes. Crosstown buses run all night.

  • Lifestyle

    Riverside Park runs, Sunday markets, Lincoln Center concerts and the Natural History Museum.

  • Safety

    Crime rates sit well below the Manhattan average, with busy, well-lit avenues late into the evening.

Location

Boundaries: West 59th Street to West 110th Street, between Central Park and the Hudson River.
