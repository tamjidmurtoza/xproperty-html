FAQ

Got questions? We've Got Answers!

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your dashboard. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period.

What happens to my listings if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. If you have more active listings than your new plan allows, the oldest ones are paused until you free up a slot or upgrade again.

Is there a free trial?

The Basic plan is free forever with limited listings, and paid plans come with a 14-day free trial. You are only charged if you keep the plan after the trial ends.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards. Annual plans can also be paid by bank transfer. You will find every invoice under Billing in your dashboard.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Cancel from the Billing page with one click. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and there are no cancellation fees.
