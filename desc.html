Xproperty - Property Listing & Real Estate HTML Template With Admin Dashboard

Xproperty is a modern, responsive and feature-rich real estate HTML template built for property agencies, realtors, brokers, developers and property listing platforms. It combines clean, well-structured code with a contemporary design, so you can showcase properties, attract buyers and tenants, and give every visitor a smooth experience on any device. From the first property search to the final tour booking, every step of the buyer and renter journey is designed to be clear, fast and easy to use, whether your visitors are on a phone during a viewing or on a desktop comparing homes at night.

With 10 unique home demos, 39 inner pages and four role dashboards (admin, agent, owner and buyer, 65 screens in total), Xproperty ships 114 ready-made HTML pages. Whether you are launching a real estate agency website, a property rental marketplace, a new development showcase or a single property sales page, this real estate template adapts to your business instead of the other way around. Choose a homepage for luxury homes, rentals, commercial units, apartment complexes or a single listing, then add the inner pages you need: property listings for sale and rent, property details, agents and agencies, neighborhood guides, new projects, open houses, home valuation, rental applications, pricing plans, a blog and more.

Xproperty is more than a static HTML template mockup. Property filters, half-map search, property compare, saved homes, the mortgage calculator and the contact form all work out of the box, so your clients see a real property website from day one. Visitors can filter listings by price, type, beds, baths and amenities, browse homes on a live map with price markers, save a search with alerts, compare up to four properties side by side and schedule a tour in person or by video chat. The property details page adds a photo gallery, video tour, floor plans, price history, currency switcher, nearby places and reviews, giving buyers everything they need to make a decision.

Behind the public site, four role dashboards turn Xproperty into a complete real estate platform. Admins approve listings, manage users, agents, subscriptions, invoices and support tickets. Agents publish properties with a multi-step wizard and track leads, tour requests, offers and listing analytics. Owners manage tenants, leases, rental applications and maintenance requests, while buyers keep their saved homes, searches, tours, offers and applications in one place. Every screen supports light and dark modes and full RTL layouts, and is built on Bootstrap 5 with SCSS source, so it is easy to rebrand and ready to connect to WordPress, Laravel, React or any backend you choose.
