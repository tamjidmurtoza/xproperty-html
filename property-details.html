Description

Welcome home! This beautifully renovated property, updated in 2010, boasts a newer roof, HVAC system, and recessed LED lighting. Step inside to a spacious open floor plan with modern flooring, iron railings, and a large kitchen featuring stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, white shaker cabinets, and a sleek backsplash. A half bathroom and laundry area are located at the rear. The finished lower level serves as an extra living space or bonus bedroom. Upstairs, you'll find three bedrooms and a stunning bathroom with full tiling, a blue vanity, and a glass sliding shower door. Outside, enjoy the privacy of a cozy backyard. Conveniently located just 5 minutes from I-95, with popular spots like Caphe Roasters, Cantina La Martina (James Beard nominee), Nemi, and Circles & Squares Pizza nearby. The home is close to Port Richmond’s business district, offering plenty of shopping and dining options. Currently tenant-occupied, this home has had consistent renters since 2020 with a monthly rent of $1,230 (potential to increase to $1,560 as per PHA FMR for a 3-bedroom). Eligible for first-time buyer grants, reach out to our in-house lender for more information. One of the owners is a licensed PA realtor. Photos show the property post-renovation, prior to tenant occupancy, with 24-hour notice required for viewings.
