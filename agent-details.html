About Alex

Alex has helped more than 120 families buy, sell and rent homes across Manhattan and Brooklyn over the last 12 years. Before joining Xproperty he worked in residential development, so he reads a floor plan, a survey and a contract with the same care.

Clients work with Alex for honest pricing advice, sharp negotiation and a calm, organised process from the first viewing to the closing table. He specialises in family homes, luxury condos and first-time buyers.
