Questions Sellers Ask Us Most

How accurate is the valuation?

Your agent compares your home with closed sales from the last six months on nearby streets, then adjusts for size, condition and outdoor space. Most of our listings sell within 3% of the valuation price.

Is the home valuation really free?

Yes. The valuation and the visit are free and there is no obligation to list with us. You keep the written report either way.

How long does it take to sell?

In 2026 our listings received an accepted offer in 24 days on average. Closing usually follows four to eight weeks later, depending on the buyer's mortgage.

What fees do you charge?

You pay a success-based commission only when the sale completes. Photography, video, the featured listing and open houses are included at no extra cost.

Should I renovate before selling?

Usually not. Small fixes, fresh paint and good staging bring the best return. Your agent will tell you which jobs are worth doing and which to skip.
