Overview

Skyline Residences is a pair of slender glass towers rising over the Downtown Dubai boulevard, a short walk from the Dubai Mall and the Opera District. The 412 homes range from compact one-bedroom apartments to full-floor penthouses, each with floor-to-ceiling windows, private balconies and open views of the skyline or the fountain lake.

Residents share a landscaped podium with an infinity pool, a fully equipped gym, a children's play area and a rooftop lounge on level 48. Homes are delivered with fitted kitchens, built-in wardrobes and a smart home system. A flexible payment plan spreads 40% of the price over the construction period, with the balance due at handover in Q4 2027.
